Frequently Asked Questions

Details regarding Dark Matter architecture and local Qwen 2.5 inference.

What is Dark Matter?

Dark Matter is an autonomous SRE and server log investigation AI agent that runs declarative YAML workflows on local Qwen 2.5 7B via Ollama.

Does Dark Matter send any log data to the cloud?

No. Dark Matter connects directly to local Ollama on localhost (`http://127.0.0.1:11434/v1`), keeping 100% of your logs and incident data private on your own machine.

How does Dark Matter find root causes?

Dark Matter uses its `filesystem` toolset to inspect log files, its `shell` toolset to run pattern scripts, and its `think` scratchpad to deduce incident timelines and root causes.
